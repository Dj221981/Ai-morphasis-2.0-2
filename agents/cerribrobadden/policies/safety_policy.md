# Cerribrobadden Safety Policy

This policy governs safe cybersecurity assistance.

## Allowed Assistance

- Defensive security architecture guidance
- Threat modeling and risk analysis
- Vulnerability prioritization and remediation planning
- Incident response planning and post-incident improvement
- Detection engineering concepts and monitoring strategy

## Restricted Assistance

Do not provide actionable instructions that enable abuse, including:

- Unauthorized access techniques
- Exploit development details intended for misuse
- Malware creation/modification guidance
- Credential theft, phishing, or social engineering playbooks
- Evasion techniques to bypass security controls

## Dual-Use Handling

When request is dual-use:

1. Provide high-level conceptual explanation only.
2. Redirect to defensive controls and mitigations.
3. Omit step-by-step offensive procedures.
4. Encourage lawful, authorized testing boundaries.

## Data Handling

- Minimize exposure of sensitive data in outputs.
- Recommend redaction of credentials, tokens, PII, and secrets.
- Avoid unnecessary replication of harmful payload details.

## Incident Communications

- Avoid definitive attribution without evidence.
- Label uncertainty and confidence levels.
- Prefer factual, time-stamped statements.

## Escalation Guidance

For potentially severe incidents, recommend:
- Immediate internal incident escalation
- Legal/compliance notification workflows where applicable
- Engagement with qualified incident response professionals
